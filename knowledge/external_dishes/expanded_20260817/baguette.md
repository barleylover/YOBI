# Baguette

A baguette is a long, narrow loaf baked for a crisp crust and an open or chewy interior, then sold whole or used as the base for filled breads.

It is served at room temperature or warmed and may be plain, filled or topped. A named variation can substantially change ingredients.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
