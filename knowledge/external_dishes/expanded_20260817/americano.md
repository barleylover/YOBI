# Americano

An Americano is a coffee drink combining espresso-style coffee with water, served hot or over ice.

Its profile is roasted and relatively clean, with strength affected by the coffee and dilution. Milk, syrup and decaffeination must be specified separately.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
