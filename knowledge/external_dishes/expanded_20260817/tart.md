# Tart

A tart is a baked pastry shell holding a sweet or savoury filling, with fruit, custard, chocolate and cheese-style fillings among common dessert forms.

It combines a crisp or crumbly shell with a soft filling and is served cool, warm or at room temperature. Ingredients are product-specific.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
