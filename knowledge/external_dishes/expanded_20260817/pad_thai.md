# Pad Thai

Pad Thai is a Thai stir-fried rice-noodle dish balancing savoury, sweet and tangy seasoning with optional protein and garnishes.

It is served warm with chewy noodles and contrasting crunchy additions. Peanut, seafood and heat must be verified per menu.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
