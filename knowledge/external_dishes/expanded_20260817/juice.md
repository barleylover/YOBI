# Fruit juice

Juice is a non-alcoholic drink made from or flavoured around fruit or vegetables, served alone or blended with other components.

It is normally cool and can be sweet, tart, pulpy or clear. Added sugar, dairy and exact fruit content are product-specific.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
