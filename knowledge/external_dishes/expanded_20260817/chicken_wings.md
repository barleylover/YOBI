# Chicken wings

Chicken wings are portioned wing pieces cooked until browned or crisp and served plain, seasoned or coated with sauce.

They are generally hot, savoury and tender, sometimes spicy or sweet. Cooking method, coating and heat level vary by menu.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
