# Loaf bread

Loaf bread is a sliced or sliceable bread baked in a pan, ranging from lean sandwich bread to soft enriched styles.

It is usually mild and soft, eaten at room temperature or toasted. Wheat, dairy, egg and sweeteners must be checked per product.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
