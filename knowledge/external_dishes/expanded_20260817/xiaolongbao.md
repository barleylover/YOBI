# Xiaolongbao

Xiaolongbao are Chinese steamed dumplings with a thin wrapper enclosing a savoury filling and hot broth-like juices.

They are served hot with a tender wrapper. Filling, broth and allergens require product-specific confirmation.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
