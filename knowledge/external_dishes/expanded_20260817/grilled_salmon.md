# Salmon steak

Salmon steak is a thick salmon portion cooked as a central plate, usually by grilling, pan cooking or roasting.

It is served warm with a browned surface and tender, flaky interior. Sauce, seasoning and doneness are product-specific.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
