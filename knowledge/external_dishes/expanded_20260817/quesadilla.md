# Quesadilla

A quesadilla is a folded or layered tortilla cooked with a melting filling and optional additions.

It is commonly served warm with a crisped exterior and soft, rich centre. Cheese is common but actual ingredients and alternatives require merchant confirmation.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
