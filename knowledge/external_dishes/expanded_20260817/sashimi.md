# Sashimi

Sashimi is a Japanese serving style centred on carefully cut raw seafood or other designated ingredients without vinegared sushi rice.

It is served cool, with a clean flavour and tender or firm texture depending on the item. Raw handling, species and freshness require merchant confirmation.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
