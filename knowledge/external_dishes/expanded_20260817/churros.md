# Churros

Churros are ridged lengths of dough cooked until browned and commonly finished with sugar or served with a dip.

They are best known for a hot, crisp exterior and soft or chewy centre. Coatings and fryer handling vary.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
