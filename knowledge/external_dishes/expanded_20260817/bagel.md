# Bagel

A bagel is a ring-shaped bread traditionally boiled briefly before baking, producing a close crumb and chewy crust.

It is eaten at room temperature, warm or toasted, with savoury or sweet toppings. Fillings and spreads are not implied by the bread name.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
