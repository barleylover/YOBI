# Takoyaki

Takoyaki is a Japanese round griddle snack made from batter cooked in moulds, traditionally with small octopus pieces and savoury toppings.

It is served hot with a soft interior and browned exterior, often tasting savoury and sauce-forward. A merchant may vary fillings and toppings.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
