# Waffle

A waffle is a griddled batter food cooked between patterned plates, served plain or with sweet or savoury toppings.

It is warm, crisp at the ridges and soft inside. Toppings, dairy, egg and wheat depend on the product.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
