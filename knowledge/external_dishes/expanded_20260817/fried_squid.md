# Fried squid

Fried squid is cut squid coated and cooked until browned, appearing as rings, strips or larger pieces.

It is hot and crisp outside with a chewy, tender centre. Batter and fryer details vary by restaurant.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
