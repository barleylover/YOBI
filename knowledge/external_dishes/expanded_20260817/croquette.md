# Croquette

A croquette is a shaped snack containing a soft filling inside a crumbed or dough-like exterior, then cooked until browned.

It is served warm with a crisp outside and soft centre. Potato, meat, curry and cream fillings are possibilities, not guarantees.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
