# Malatang

Malatang is a Chinese hot-pot-style bowl in which selected ingredients are cooked in a seasoned broth associated with chilli heat and numbing spice.

It is served steaming hot and can be intensely aromatic or spicy. Broth, ingredient selection and heat vary by restaurant.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
