# Yakisoba

Yakisoba is a Japanese stir-fried noodle dish seasoned with a savoury, lightly sweet sauce and optional vegetables or protein.

It is served warm with chewy noodles and browned aromas. Protein and toppings are not fixed.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
