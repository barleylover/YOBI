# Acai bowl

An acai bowl is a chilled, thick fruit-based bowl topped with fruit, grains, seeds or other additions.

It is cool, smooth and commonly sweet or tart with contrasting crunchy toppings. Exact base, sugar and toppings vary.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
