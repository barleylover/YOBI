# Caffe latte

A caffe latte is an espresso-style coffee drink combined with a larger proportion of milk or a milk alternative.

It is smooth and creamy, served hot or iced, and may include flavouring. The name does not identify dairy type or added sugar.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
