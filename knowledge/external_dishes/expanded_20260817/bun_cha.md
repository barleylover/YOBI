# Bun cha

Bun cha is a Vietnamese meal pairing rice noodles, grilled components, herbs and a dipping broth or sauce.

It combines warm grilled aromas with cool noodles and fresh textures. Meat and sauce composition vary by restaurant.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
