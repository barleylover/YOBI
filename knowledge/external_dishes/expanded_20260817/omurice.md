# Omurice

Omurice is a Japanese modern comfort dish pairing seasoned rice with a cooked egg covering and sauce.

It is served warm, soft and savoury, often with sweet-tangy sauce. Rice seasoning, filling and egg style vary.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
