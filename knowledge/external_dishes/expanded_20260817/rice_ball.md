# Korean rice ball

Jumeokbap is a Korean hand-formed rice snack or side in which seasoned rice is shaped into compact portions, sometimes with mix-ins.

It is usually soft and chewy, served warm or at room temperature, and can be mild or savoury. Mix-ins are not guaranteed.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
