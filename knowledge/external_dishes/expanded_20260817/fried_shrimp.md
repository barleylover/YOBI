# Fried shrimp

Fried shrimp is shrimp coated in batter or crumbs and cooked until the exterior becomes crisp.

It is served hot with a firm, tender centre and savoury crust. Coating, oil and shared-fryer exposure require merchant confirmation.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
