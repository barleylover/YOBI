# Nasi goreng

Nasi goreng is an Indonesian fried-rice family seasoned for a deeply savoury, aromatic profile and served with varied toppings.

It is served hot with separated, stir-fried grains. Sweetness, chilli, protein and garnishes differ by recipe.
