# Pastry

Pastry is a broad baked family using layered, crumbly or enriched dough to create sweet and savoury products.

Textures range from flaky and crisp to soft and rich. Filling, dairy, egg, wheat and nuts cannot be inferred from the family name.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
