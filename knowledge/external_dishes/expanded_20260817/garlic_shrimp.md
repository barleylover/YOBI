# Garlic shrimp

Garlic shrimp is a cooked shrimp dish seasoned around garlic, commonly served as a plate or bowl component.

It is warm, savoury and aromatic with a firm, tender bite. Sauce, butter, spice and side dishes differ by menu.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
