# Castella

Castella is a soft sponge-style cake with a fine, moist crumb, adapted across Japanese and Korean bakery traditions.

It is sweet, tender and generally served at room temperature. Flavour additions and ingredient details differ by bakery.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
