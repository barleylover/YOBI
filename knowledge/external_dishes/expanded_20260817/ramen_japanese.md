# Japanese ramen

Japanese ramen is a noodle-soup family pairing wheat noodles with a seasoned broth and varied toppings.

It is served steaming hot and ranges from clean and light to thick, rich or spicy. Broth base and toppings are menu-specific.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
