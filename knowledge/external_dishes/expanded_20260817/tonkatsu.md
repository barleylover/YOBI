# Tonkatsu

Tonkatsu is a Japanese breaded pork cutlet cooked until the coating is crisp and the meat is tender.

It is served warm with a savoury crust and commonly a tangy sauce. Cut, oil and accompaniments depend on the restaurant.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
