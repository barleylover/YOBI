# Ice cream

Ice cream is a frozen dessert family churned or otherwise frozen to create a smooth, scoopable texture, with dairy and non-dairy styles both available.

It is served frozen, sweet and creamy or icy depending on the formulation. The name alone does not prove dairy content or allergen safety.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
