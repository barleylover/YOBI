# Cup-bap

Cup-bap is a Korean portable rice-meal format layering rice with compact toppings and sauce in a cup or bowl.

It is usually served warm and savoury, with texture and heat determined by the topping. No protein or sauce is guaranteed by the format.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
