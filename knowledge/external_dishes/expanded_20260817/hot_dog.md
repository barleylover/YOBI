# Hot dog

Hot dog is a portable bread-and-filling format; Korean menus may also use the name for battered, skewered corn-dog styles.

It is commonly served hot with a soft or crisp exterior and savoury centre. The format does not identify sausage type, batter or cheese.

This is general culinary guidance, not a statement about one merchant's recipe, ingredients, certification, allergens, portion or menu-specific spice level. Check the current merchant information before ordering.
