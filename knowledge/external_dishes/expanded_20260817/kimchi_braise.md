# Kimchi-jjim

Kimchi-jjim is a Korean braised dish that slowly cooks mature kimchi with liquid and optional additions until deeply softened.

It is served hot with a tender texture and robust savoury, sour and often spicy flavour. Protein and heat level vary by recipe.
