# Kimchi fried rice

Kimchi fried rice is a Korean rice dish in which cooked rice and kimchi are stir-fried together. Its familiar character is savoury, tangy and often moderately spicy, although the exact balance varies.

The rice is usually served hot. Stir-frying can leave some grains lightly crisp while the overall texture remains soft and hearty.

Egg, meat, cheese and seaweed are common additions, but none is guaranteed for a particular restaurant menu. The menu and merchant must confirm actual ingredients and dietary suitability.
