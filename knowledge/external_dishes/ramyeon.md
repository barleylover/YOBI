# Korean ramyeon

Korean ramyeon is a hot noodle dish built around springy noodles and seasoned broth. Many familiar versions are savoury and chilli-forward, while mild varieties also exist.

The noodles are soft yet chewy and are usually served steaming hot. Eggs, vegetables, dumplings, rice cakes and cheese are possible additions.

Brand, seasoning, toppings and spice intensity vary. This Wiki entry supplies only general cultural and sensory context, not restaurant-specific ingredient facts.
