# Bossam

Bossam is a Korean dish commonly built around tender slices of boiled or gently simmered pork served for wrapping. The usual eating style pairs the meat with leaves and strongly seasoned accompaniments.

Its central texture is soft and succulent rather than crisp. The meat itself is savoury and mild, while kimchi or sauces can add saltiness, sweetness and heat.

This is a general dish description, not a claim about one shop's recipe, portion or side dishes. Actual ingredients, sauces and dietary details require merchant confirmation.
