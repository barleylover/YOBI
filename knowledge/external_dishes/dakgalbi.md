# Dakgalbi

Dakgalbi is a Korean chicken dish commonly stir-fried with vegetables in a chilli-forward sauce. Its general profile is savoury, spicy and slightly sweet.

It is normally eaten hot. Tender chicken and softer vegetables contrast with chewy additions such as rice cakes when those are included.

Cheese, noodles and other additions are variations rather than guaranteed contents. A restaurant's menu remains the authority for its actual ingredients and spice intensity.
