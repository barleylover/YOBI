# Jokbal

Jokbal is a Korean pork dish made by long braising, producing slices with a glossy savoury character. Aromatic seasoning often gives a gently sweet and salty profile.

The texture combines tender meat with a springy, chewy outer layer. It is commonly shared warm or at room temperature with vegetable wraps and condiments.

Preparation liquids, accompaniments and allergens vary by merchant. This general Wiki entry cannot establish the ingredients or safety of an individual menu.
