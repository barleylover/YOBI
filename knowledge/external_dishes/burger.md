# Burger

A burger is a bread-based meal built around a patty or other central filling in a split bun. Its usual character is savoury and hearty, with sauces and toppings adding sweetness, acidity or heat.

It is often served warm. A soft bun, browned filling and crisp vegetables can create contrasting textures.

Beef, chicken, fish and plant-based versions all exist, so the dish name does not prove a main ingredient or dietary status. Use merchant evidence for those details.
