# Sushi

Sushi is a Japanese dish family centred on seasoned rice paired with toppings or fillings. The flavour often combines mild sweetness, gentle acidity and savoury seafood or vegetables.

It is commonly served cool or near room temperature. Soft rice can contrast with tender fish, crisp vegetables or chewy seaweed.

Raw and cooked varieties both exist, and a menu name alone cannot establish ingredients, allergens or handling. Merchant information is required for those facts.
