# Budae-jjigae

Budae-jjigae is a Korean hot stew known for a rich, savoury and chilli-seasoned broth. Processed meats, noodles, beans and vegetables are common components of the dish family.

It is served bubbling hot, with soft ingredients and a thick, hearty eating experience. Exact saltiness and spice vary considerably.
