# Bread

Bread is a baked food family based on dough, with products ranging from mild savoury loaves to filled sweet buns. Baking creates a soft interior and can produce a crisp or chewy crust.

Bread may be eaten warm, cool or at room temperature. Fillings, toppings and enriched doughs can substantially change flavour and texture.

The broad family name does not establish wheat, dairy, egg, nuts or other ingredients for a particular item. Product and merchant details remain authoritative.
