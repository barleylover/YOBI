# Sandwich

A sandwich is a broad food family in which bread holds a savoury or sweet filling. It can be light and crisp or warm, rich and filling depending on the bread and contents.

Common textures include soft bread, crunchy vegetables and toasted surfaces. Sandwiches may be served cool, at room temperature or hot.

The family definition does not identify meat, dairy, egg, sauces or allergens in a particular product. Those details must come from the menu or merchant.
