# Cake

Cake is a sweet baked dessert family with styles ranging from light sponge to dense, rich layers. Cream, fruit, chocolate and nuts are common flavour directions rather than guaranteed contents.

Most cakes are soft, with frostings or fillings adding creamy or chewy contrasts. They are generally served cool or at room temperature.

Recipes and allergens vary widely. This general description cannot confirm dairy, egg, wheat, nuts or dietary suitability for an individual menu.
