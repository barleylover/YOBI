# Samgyeopsal

Samgyeopsal is Korean pork belly prepared on a grill or hot plate. Browning gives the meat a rich savoury character and can create crisp edges around a tender centre.

It is typically served hot and eaten with wraps, vegetables and sauces. Those accompaniments shape the final saltiness and heat.

This entry describes the general dish family only. Cut, seasoning, doneness, sides and dietary details differ by merchant and must be checked separately.
