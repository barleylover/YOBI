# Tangsuyuk

## Overview
Tangsuyuk is a Korean-Chinese family of crisp battered pork pieces served with a glossy sweet-and-sour vegetable sauce.

## Taste
It is sweet, tangy and savory, with mild fruit or vinegar notes.

## Texture
Crisp, chewy-coated pork contrasts with smooth sauce and lightly cooked vegetables.

## Temperature
It is served hot, while sauce may arrive separately or already poured.

## Satiety
It is a rich shared main, commonly paired with noodles or fried rice.

## Culture
Tangsuyuk is a classic Korean-Chinese group-order side and is known for the debate over dipping versus pouring sauce.

## Analogy
It is crispy pork paired with a Korean-style translucent sweet-and-sour sauce.

## Ingredients
Pork, starch coating, frying oil and sweet-and-sour sauce define the family. Wheat, egg, soy or seafood seasoning can appear.

## Safety
Wheat, soy, egg and shellfish risk are possible through coating, sauce and shared wok or fryer handling.
