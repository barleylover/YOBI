# Spicy tangsuyuk

## Character and experience

Spicy tangsuyuk is a tangsuyuk variant whose sweet-and-sour sauce deliberately adds chili heat.

It is sweet, tart, savory and hot, with intensity varying by recipe.

Crisp coated pork meets sticky sauce and vegetables.

It is served hot and the sauce softens the coating over time.

It is a rich shared main or substantial side.

## Context and comparisons

Often labeled Sichuan-style in Korean-Chinese menus, it is a familiar flavor variation rather than a separate merchant invention.

It is tangsuyuk with chili heat integrated into its sauce.

## Ingredients and variations

Pork, starch coating and spicy sweet-and-sour sauce define the variant. Peppers, garlic and sesame may appear.

Wheat, soy, egg, shellfish and sesame are possible through batter, sauce and shared preparation.
