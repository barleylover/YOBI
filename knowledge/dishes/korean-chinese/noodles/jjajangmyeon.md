# Jjajangmyeon

## Character and experience

Jjajangmyeon is Korean-Chinese wheat noodles topped with a glossy sauce made from fried black bean paste and diced ingredients.

The sauce is deep, savory, slightly sweet and roasted, without the chilli heat implied by its dark color.

Springy noodles are coated by a thick sauce with soft onion and diced meat or vegetables.

It is served hot and should be mixed promptly so the sauce coats the noodles evenly.

A bowl is a filling one-person meal; set menus often add a fried side.

## Context and comparisons

It is a classic Korean delivery food and is strongly associated with moving day and casual family meals.

Think of thick wheat noodles in a rich, mildly sweet fermented black-bean gravy.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat noodles, black bean paste and onion are core. Pork is common but vegetarian and seafood variations exist.

Wheat is presumed present; soy and pork are common risks. A dark sauce should never be treated as proof of a meat-free recipe.
