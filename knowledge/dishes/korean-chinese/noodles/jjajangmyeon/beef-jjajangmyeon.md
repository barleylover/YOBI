# Beef jjajangmyeon

## Overview
Beef jjajangmyeon is a jjajangmyeon variant that names beef as the prominent meat in the black-bean sauce.

## Taste
It is roasted, savory and mildly sweet, with a deeper beef note.

## Texture
Springy noodles carry thick sauce with tender beef and softened onion.

## Temperature
It is served hot.

## Satiety
Noodles and beef make it a substantial one-bowl meal.

## Culture
It is a reusable Korean-Chinese variation that distinguishes beef from the more common unspecified or pork-based sauce.

## Analogy
It is classic black-bean noodles with beef made explicit as the defining protein.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, black bean paste and beef define the variant. Onion, oil and egg garnish may appear.

## Safety
Wheat and soy are presumed present. Egg and shellfish risk are possible through noodles, garnish and shared preparation.
