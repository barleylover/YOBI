# Gan-jjajang

## Overview
Gan-jjajang is a jjajangmyeon variant in which the black-bean sauce is stir-fried more directly and commonly served separately for the diner to mix.

## Taste
It is intensely roasted, savory and mildly sweet, often less diluted than standard jjajang sauce.

## Texture
Springy noodles are coated by a thick chunky sauce with onion and optional pork or seafood.

## Temperature
The noodles and freshly fried sauce are served hot.

## Satiety
A bowl is a substantial one-dish meal.

## Culture
Gan-jjajang is a long-established Korean-Chinese menu subtype distinguished by preparation, not a merchant-specific product.

## Analogy
It is jjajangmyeon with a concentrated wok-fried sauce mixed at the table.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, black bean paste and cooking oil define the variant. Onion, pork, seafood and egg garnish can appear.

## Safety
Wheat and soy are presumed present. Shellfish and egg are possible through toppings and shared preparation.
