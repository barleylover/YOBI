# Pork jjamppong

## Overview
Pork jjamppong is a jjamppong variant that makes pork the prominent named protein instead of centering mixed seafood.

## Taste
It is smoky, savory, meaty and chili-hot.

## Texture
Springy noodles and tender pork sit with softened vegetables in broth.

## Temperature
It is served very hot.

## Satiety
Pork and noodles make it a filling one-dish meal.

## Culture
Meat-forward jjamppong is a stable Korean-Chinese menu subtype that contrasts with seafood-centered bowls.

## Analogy
It is spicy jjamppong built around wok-fried pork rather than a mixed shellfish topping.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, pork, broth and chili seasoning define the variant. Cabbage, onion, soy sauce and seafood stock may appear.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Soy, egg and shellfish risk are possible through noodles, seasoning and shared wok preparation.
