# Jjamppong

## Overview
Jjamppong is a Korean-Chinese family of wheat-noodle soups characterized by a boldly seasoned broth, commonly red and spicy, with vegetables and seafood or meat.

## Taste
It is savory, smoky and chili-hot, often with briny seafood depth.

## Texture
Springy noodles sit in broth with tender seafood, meat and softened-crisp vegetables.

## Temperature
It is served very hot.

## Satiety
A large bowl is a substantial one-dish meal.

## Culture
Jjamppong is one of the central Korean-Chinese delivery and restaurant noodle categories alongside jjajangmyeon.

## Analogy
It is a wok-aromatic spicy noodle soup whose protein mix defines the subtype.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, broth and chili seasoning define the family. Cabbage, onion, shellfish, squid, pork and egg noodles can appear.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Shellfish, fish, soy and egg are possible and must be resolved by the named variant or menu.
