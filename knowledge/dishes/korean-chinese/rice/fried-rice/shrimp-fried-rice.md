# Shrimp fried rice

## Character and experience

Shrimp fried rice is a Korean-Chinese fried-rice variant whose named protein is shrimp.

It is savory, lightly sweet and smoky, with mild briny notes from shrimp.

Firm rice grains, springy shrimp and soft optional egg create distinct bites.

It is served hot.

A plate is a filling one-dish meal.

## Context and comparisons

It is a familiar Korean-Chinese restaurant and delivery rice option.

It is wok-fried rice built around shrimp rather than an unspecified mixed topping.

## Ingredients and variations

Rice, shrimp and cooking oil define the variant. Egg, vegetables, soy seasoning and jjajang sauce are possible.

Shellfish risk is presumed present. Egg, soy and wheat are possible through stir-fry seasoning and accompanying sauce.
