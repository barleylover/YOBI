# Jjajang fried rice

## Overview
Jjajang fried rice is a Korean-Chinese fried-rice variant defined by a serving of roasted black-bean sauce.

## Taste
It is savory, roasted and gently sweet, with light wok smokiness.

## Texture
Separate fried rice grains contrast with thick smooth or chunky jjajang sauce.

## Temperature
It is served hot.

## Satiety
Rice, oil and sauce make it a filling plate.

## Culture
It is a standard bridge between the fried-rice and jjajang categories in Korean-Chinese restaurants.

## Analogy
It replaces jjajangmyeon's noodles with wok-fried rice.

## Ingredients
Rice, cooking oil and black bean sauce define the variant. Egg, vegetables, pork or seafood can appear.

## Safety
Soy is presumed present. Wheat, egg and shellfish risk are possible through sauce, garnish and shared wok preparation.
