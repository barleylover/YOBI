# Korean-Chinese fried rice

## Overview
Korean-Chinese fried rice is a family of wok-fried rice dishes served with egg, vegetables and optional meat or seafood, often with jjajang sauce or soup.

## Taste
It is savory, lightly smoky and oil-rich, with sauce adding salty roasted sweetness.

## Texture
Separate fried rice grains mix with soft egg and diced toppings.

## Temperature
It is served hot.

## Satiety
A plate is a filling one-dish meal.

## Culture
It is a standard rice alternative in Korean-Chinese restaurants and delivery menus.

## Analogy
It is wok-fried rice with Korean-Chinese sides and seasoning conventions.

## Ingredients
Rice and cooking oil define the family. Egg, vegetables, pork, seafood, soy seasoning and jjajang sauce vary.

## Safety
Egg, soy, wheat and shellfish risk are possible. The family name does not identify the protein or accompanying sauce.
