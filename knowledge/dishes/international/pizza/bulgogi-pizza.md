# Bulgogi pizza

## Overview
Bulgogi pizza is a Korean pizza variant whose named topping is sweet-savory marinated beef.

## Taste
It is savory, mildly sweet and cheesy, with soy, garlic and optional sesame notes.

## Texture
Tender beef and melted cheese sit over a crisp-chewy crust.

## Temperature
It is served hot or warm.

## Satiety
Beef, cheese and bread make it a rich, filling meal.

## Culture
It adapts Korean bulgogi flavor to the delivery-pizza format and is a long-running local menu category.

## Analogy
It is a cheese pizza topped like a bulgogi rice dish, without the rice.

## Ingredients
Wheat dough, beef and cheese define the variant. Soy marinade, onion, mushrooms and sesame may appear.

## Safety
Wheat and milk are presumed present. Soy, egg and sesame are possible in marinade, dough and sauce.
