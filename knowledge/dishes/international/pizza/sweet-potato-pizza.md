# Sweet potato pizza

## Character and experience

Sweet potato pizza is a Korean delivery-pizza variant defined by sweet potato pieces, mash or mousse used as a topping or crust ring.

It combines sweet, earthy sweet potato with salty cheese and savory crust.

Creamy mash or soft chunks contrast with melted cheese and chewy baked dough.

It is served hot or warm.

The starch-rich topping and cheese make it especially filling.

## Context and comparisons

Sweet potato pizza is a familiar Korean pizza-shop category reflecting the local preference for sweet-savory toppings.

It is cheese pizza with sweet potato functioning as a creamy signature topping.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat dough, sweet potato and cheese define the variant. Dairy cream, corn, meat or sweet sauce may be added.

Wheat and milk are presumed present. Soy and egg are possible through dough, mousse and sauces.
