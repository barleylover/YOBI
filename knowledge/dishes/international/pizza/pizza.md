# Pizza

## Overview
The demo pizza family is a baked wheat-dough base topped with cheese and a changing set of sauces and toppings.

## Taste
It is savory and rich, with sweetness, spice and smoke determined by sauce and toppings.

## Texture
A crisp or chewy crust supports melted cheese and softer toppings.

## Temperature
Pizza is delivered hot but remains commonly eaten warm or at room temperature.

## Satiety
It is usually a shareable meal, although personal sizes exist.

## Culture
Korean delivery pizza often uses generous, locally adapted toppings and half-and-half combinations.

## Analogy
The basic format is familiar internationally; the useful YOBI explanation focuses on topping composition, size and Korean-style flavor combinations.

## Ingredients
Wheat dough and cheese are defining or core in the demo family. Tomato sauce and meat toppings vary by product.

## Safety
Wheat and milk are presumed present. Pork and other animal toppings require menu-level facts, and half-and-half pizzas inherit risks from both sides.
