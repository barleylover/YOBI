# Beef gukbap

## Character and experience

Beef gukbap is a gukbap variant built around cooked beef, broth and rice served together or combined at the table.

It is deeply savory and meaty, with garlic, scallion, pepper or chili seasoning varying by regional and shop style.

Tender beef and soft rice sit in a fluid broth, sometimes with crisp scallion or vegetables.

It is served steaming hot and retains heat well in a stone or metal bowl.

Rice, broth and beef make it a filling one-bowl meal.

## Context and comparisons

Beef gukbap is everyday Korean comfort food associated with quick, warming meals rather than formal multi-course dining.

It is a beef soup and rice meal integrated into one bowl.

## Ingredients and variations

Beef, broth and rice define the variant. Garlic, scallion, radish, soy seasoning and chili are possible.

Soy, wheat and sesame are possible through seasoning or garnishes. Beef is presumed present, while a specific stock recipe remains merchant-specific.
