# Kimchi gukbap

## Character and experience

Kimchi gukbap is a gukbap variant in which kimchi flavors the broth and accompanies rice as the defining component.

It is tangy, savory and usually spicy, with fermented depth.

Soft rice and tender kimchi sit in broth with optional tofu or pork.

It is served very hot.

Rice and robust broth make it a filling comfort meal.

## Context and comparisons

Kimchi gukbap is a familiar home-style and late-night soup-rice format distinct from a thicker kimchi stew set.

It is a brothy kimchi-and-rice bowl rather than a separate stew with rice beside it.

## Ingredients and variations

Kimchi, broth and rice define the variant. Pork, tofu, egg and seafood seasoning may appear.

Fish or shellfish can enter through kimchi and stock, while soy, wheat and sesame are possible through seasoning.
