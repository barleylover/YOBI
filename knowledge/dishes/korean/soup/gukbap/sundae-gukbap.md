# Sundae gukbap

## Overview
Sundae gukbap is a gukbap variant containing Korean blood sausage, usually with pork broth and optional pork offal, served with rice.

## Taste
The broth is savory and pork-rich, often seasoned at the table with salted shrimp, chili paste, chives or perilla powder.

## Texture
Soft rice, springy sausage casing, tender filling and varied pork pieces create a hearty mixed texture.

## Temperature
It is served very hot and commonly arrives bubbling.

## Satiety
The combination of rice, sausage and broth is a substantial full meal.

## Culture
It is a widely recognized Korean soup-house dish and a common restorative or late-night meal.

## Analogy
It is a pork soup-and-rice bowl whose identifying component is Korean blood sausage.

## Ingredients
Korean blood sausage, pork, broth and rice define the variant. Offal, chives, garlic and seasonings differ by shop.

## Safety
Pork is presumed present. Soy, wheat and sesame are possible in sausage, broth or condiments; ingredient details for a specific sausage are often incomplete.
