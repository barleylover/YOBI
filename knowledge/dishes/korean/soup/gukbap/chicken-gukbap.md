# Chicken gukbap

## Overview
Chicken gukbap is a gukbap variant built around cooked chicken, chicken broth and rice.

## Taste
It is savory and warming, ranging from mild and clean to peppery or chili-spiced.

## Texture
Tender shredded or sliced chicken and soft rice sit in broth.

## Temperature
It is served steaming hot.

## Satiety
Chicken, broth and rice make it a filling one-bowl meal.

## Culture
Chicken gukbap is a reusable Korean soup-rice category found in home-style and specialist soup menus.

## Analogy
It is chicken soup and rice integrated into one Korean-style bowl.

## Ingredients
Chicken, chicken broth and rice define the variant. Garlic, scallion, egg and sesame may appear.

## Safety
Soy, wheat, egg and sesame are possible through seasoning and garnish; chicken remains presumed present.
