# Bean sprout gukbap

## Character and experience

Bean sprout gukbap is a light gukbap variant centered on soybean sprouts, broth and rice, often finished with egg or seafood-based seasoning.

It is clean, savory and refreshing, with mild bean notes and optional chili heat.

Crisp-tender sprouts contrast with soft rice in a clear or lightly seasoned broth.

It is served very hot, commonly in an earthenware bowl.

Rice makes it a complete meal, while the sprout-forward broth feels lighter than meat-heavy gukbap.

## Context and comparisons

It is strongly associated with Jeonju-style breakfast and hangover comfort food, although preparation varies.

It is a crisp-sprout soup made substantial by rice in the bowl.

## Ingredients and variations

Soybean sprouts, broth and rice define the variant. Egg, anchovy stock, salted shrimp, garlic and chili can appear.

Soy is presumed present because soybean sprouts define the dish. Egg, fish and shellfish risk are possible through toppings and stock.
