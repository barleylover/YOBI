# Seolleongtang

## Overview
Seolleongtang is a long-simmered Korean beef-bone soup known for an opaque pale broth and restrained seasoning.

## Taste
The broth is mild, beefy and gently rich, with salt, scallion and pepper commonly adjusted at the table.

## Texture
The broth has body without cream, accompanied by tender beef, rice or thin noodles.

## Temperature
It is served very hot and designed as a warming meal.

## Satiety
With rice and meat it is a substantial one-person bowl.

## Culture
The table-seasoning ritual lets each diner control the final salt and scallion balance.

## Analogy
Think of a mild, cloudy beef-bone broth; its milky appearance comes from simmered bones, not proof of dairy.

## Ingredients
Beef-bone broth defines the family. Beef slices, rice and thin wheat noodles vary by bowl.

## Safety
The dish is not vegetarian or halal-certified merely because it is mild. Wheat is possible through noodles, while the pale color should not be mislabeled as milk content.
