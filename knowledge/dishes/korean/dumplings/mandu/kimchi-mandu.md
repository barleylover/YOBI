# Kimchi mandu

## Character and experience

Kimchi mandu is a mandu variant whose filling prominently includes chopped seasoned kimchi.

It is tangy, savory and usually spicy, with fermented depth.

A soft or crisp wrapper surrounds a moist chopped filling of kimchi and other ingredients.

It is served hot or warm, with temperature and crust depending on cooking method.

Several dumplings make a filling snack, side or light meal.

## Context and comparisons

Kimchi mandu is a stable Korean dumpling category commonly paired with soup or sold steamed and fried.

It is a Korean dumpling whose identity comes from fermented kimchi in the filling.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat wrapper, kimchi and mixed filling define the variant. Pork, tofu, glass noodles and vegetables can appear.

Wheat is presumed present. Soy, fish, shellfish, egg and sesame are possible through kimchi seasoning, filling and wrapper.
