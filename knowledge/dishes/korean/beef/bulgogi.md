# Bulgogi

## Overview
Bulgogi is thinly sliced beef cooked after marinating in a sweet-savory soy-based seasoning. This family entry describes beef bulgogi; pork versions require a separate menu fact.

## Taste
The usual profile is savory, gently sweet and aromatic, with browned edges from quick cooking.

## Texture
Thin slicing and marinade make the beef tender, while onion and mushrooms can add softer bites.

## Temperature
It is served hot and can arrive with cooking juices that keep the meat moist.

## Satiety
A serving with rice is a substantial meal; shared platters can feed more than one person.

## Culture
Bulgogi is a widely recognized Korean grilled or pan-cooked meat dish used in home meals, restaurants and delivery bowls.

## Analogy
Think of tender strips of beef in a lightly sweet soy marinade rather than a heavily smoked barbecue.

## Ingredients
Beef and soy sauce are defining or core in this demo concept. Sugar, garlic, onion, pear and sesame are common recipe variations.

## Safety
Soy is presumed present, sesame is possible, and the marinade may include additional processed ingredients. A beef label does not establish halal handling or absence of shared equipment.
