# Korean fried chicken

## Overview
Korean fried chicken is crisp fried chicken sold plain or finished with one of many sauces or seasonings. Bone-in and boneless products may use different coatings.

## Taste
Plain versions are salty and savory; seasoned versions can be sweet, garlicky, spicy, cheesy or soy-forward.

## Texture
A thin crisp shell and moist chicken are the target, though steam in delivery packaging can soften the crust.

## Temperature
It is served hot but remains commonly eaten after cooling during delivery or sharing.

## Satiety
A whole chicken is usually shareable, while smaller boneless portions can serve one person.

## Culture
Chicken delivery is a major Korean social food, often paired with pickled radish and shared during sports or gatherings.

## Analogy
It is familiar fried chicken with unusually varied glazes, powders and half-and-half ordering styles.

## Ingredients
Chicken and frying oil are core. Flour or starch coating is common; sauces and seasoning powders depend on the exact menu.

## Safety
Wheat, soy, milk and shared frying oil are possible risks. Plain appearance does not prove an allergen-free coating or fryer.
