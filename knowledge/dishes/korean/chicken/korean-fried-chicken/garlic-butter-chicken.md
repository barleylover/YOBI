# Garlic butter chicken

## Character and experience

Garlic butter chicken is a fried-chicken variant defined by a butter-rich garlic sauce, glaze or seasoning.

It is savory, aromatic, creamy and mildly sweet or salty, usually without dominant red chili heat.

Butter sauce lightly coats crisp chicken and can soften the crust over time.

It is served hot or warm so the butter remains fluid.

The fried coating and butter sauce make it a rich shared main.

## Context and comparisons

Garlic-butter flavor is a broadly reusable Korean chicken and delivery-food category rather than a single branded item.

It is crisp chicken finished like garlic bread, with butter and garlic leading the flavor.

## Ingredients and variations

Chicken, dairy butter and garlic define the variant. Wheat coating, soy seasoning and egg batter may appear.

Milk is presumed present. Wheat, soy and egg remain possible through coating and sauce.
