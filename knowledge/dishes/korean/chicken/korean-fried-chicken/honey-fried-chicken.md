# Honey fried chicken

## Character and experience

Honey fried chicken is a Korean fried-chicken variant whose defining seasoning is a clearly sweet honey-forward glaze or powder.

It is sweet, savory and buttery or soy-like depending on the sauce, usually with low chili heat.

A sticky or powdered sweet coating sits on a crisp fried shell and moist chicken.

It is served hot or warm.

The fried coating and sweet glaze make it a rich shared main.

## Context and comparisons

Honey chicken is a stable Korean delivery flavor category for diners seeking sweetness rather than red chili heat.

It is Korean fried chicken finished with dessert-like honey sweetness while remaining savory.

## Ingredients and variations

Chicken, frying oil and honey-style sweetener define the variant. Soy sauce, butter, garlic or sesame may appear.

Wheat, soy, egg, milk and sesame are possible in coating and compound glaze.
