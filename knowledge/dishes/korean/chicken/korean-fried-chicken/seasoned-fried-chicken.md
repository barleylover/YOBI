# Seasoned fried chicken

## Character and experience

Seasoned fried chicken is Korean fried chicken finished with a coating of sauce or seasoning, most recognizably a glossy sweet-spicy red glaze.

The common profile is sweet, savory, tangy and mildly to strongly spicy, but garlic, soy and non-spicy seasoned versions also exist.

The glaze makes the crust sticky and less dry-crisp than plain fried chicken while the interior remains tender.

It is served hot or warm and remains popular after cooling, although the sauce softens the fried coating during delivery.

A full order is normally shareable, while smaller boneless portions can be a rich one-person meal.

## Context and comparisons

Yangnyeom chicken is a signature Korean delivery style and is commonly ordered beside plain chicken as a half-and-half set.

It resembles glazed fried chicken, with the exact Korean seasoning ranging from sweet red sauce to soy-garlic or powdered finishes.

## Ingredients and variations

Chicken and frying oil are inherited from Korean fried chicken, while a finishing sauce or seasoning defines this variant. Gochujang is common in red sauce but not guaranteed.

Soy and wheat are possible through glaze and coating, with milk also possible through inherited seasoning products. Sauce color or spice level cannot prove an allergen profile.
