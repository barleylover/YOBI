# Plain Korean fried chicken

## Overview
Plain Korean fried chicken is the unsauced fried-chicken variant, defined by a seasoned crisp coating rather than a post-fry glaze.

## Taste
It is savory and salty with roasted and fried notes; seasoning strength varies by shop.

## Texture
The defining contrast is crisp exterior and moist cooked chicken inside.

## Temperature
It is served hot, though delivery time can soften the crust.

## Satiety
A portion is a filling main dish commonly shared by two or more people.

## Culture
Often called huraideu, it is the baseline half of the Korean half-and-half chicken format.

## Analogy
It is the crisp, unglazed reference point from which sauced Korean chicken variants differ.

## Ingredients
Chicken and frying oil are core. Wheat flour or starch, egg, milk powder and soy-based seasoning are possible coating components.

## Safety
Wheat, egg, soy and milk are possible because coating recipes vary. Shared fryers can add cross-contact that Wiki knowledge cannot resolve.
