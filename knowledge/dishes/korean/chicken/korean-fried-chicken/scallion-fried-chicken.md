# Scallion fried chicken

## Overview
Scallion fried chicken, or padak, is a Korean fried-chicken variant defined by abundant thinly shredded green onion served over or beside the chicken.

## Taste
It is savory and fried with sharp, fresh onion notes and often a sweet-tangy soy dressing.

## Texture
Crisp chicken contrasts with cool, crisp or lightly wilted scallion strands.

## Temperature
Chicken is served hot while the scallion topping is usually cool or room temperature.

## Satiety
It is a filling shared main.

## Culture
Padak is a standard Korean chicken-shop category that uses fresh scallion to cut through fried richness.

## Analogy
It is fried chicken paired with a salad-like pile of shredded green onion as a defining topping.

## Ingredients
Chicken, frying oil and scallion define the variant. Wheat coating and soy-based dressing are common.

## Safety
Wheat, soy, egg, milk and sesame are possible through coating and dressing.
