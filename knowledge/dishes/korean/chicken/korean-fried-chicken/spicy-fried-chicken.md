# Spicy seasoned fried chicken

## Overview
Spicy seasoned fried chicken is a red-sauced Korean fried-chicken variant defined by stronger chili heat than ordinary sweet yangnyeom chicken.

## Taste
It is hot, sweet, savory and tangy, with heat intensity differing across recipes.

## Texture
A thick sauce coats the crisp shell and gradually makes it sticky and tender.

## Temperature
It is served hot or warm.

## Satiety
It is a rich, shareable main dish whose strong seasoning can feel heavier than plain chicken.

## Culture
Chicken shops commonly distinguish this hot style from both mild yangnyeom and soy-garlic flavors.

## Analogy
It is yangnyeom chicken turned toward deliberate chili heat rather than only sweet glaze.

## Ingredients
Chicken and a chili-based sauce define the variant. Flour coating, soy seasoning, garlic and sesame may be present.

## Safety
Wheat, soy, egg, milk and sesame are possible in batter and sauce. A spicy label does not identify exact allergens.
