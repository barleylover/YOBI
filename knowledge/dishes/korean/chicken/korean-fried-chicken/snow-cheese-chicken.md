# Snow cheese chicken

## Overview
Snow cheese chicken is fried chicken dusted generously with a pale cheese-flavored seasoning powder after frying.

## Taste
It is salty, creamy and slightly sweet, with dairy seasoning tempering the fried savoriness.

## Texture
Dry powder clings to the crisp crust while the chicken remains moist inside.

## Temperature
It is served hot or warm, although the powder remains noticeable after cooling.

## Satiety
The fried coating and cheese seasoning make it a rich shareable main.

## Culture
Powder-seasoned chicken is a recognizable Korean delivery category alongside plain and glazed styles.

## Analogy
It is fried chicken finished like a cheese-seasoned snack, not chicken covered in melted cheese.

## Ingredients
Chicken, frying oil and cheese seasoning define the variant. Flour, starch and additional powdered flavors depend on the recipe.

## Safety
Milk is presumed present through cheese seasoning. Wheat, egg and soy remain possible through coating and flavor blends.
