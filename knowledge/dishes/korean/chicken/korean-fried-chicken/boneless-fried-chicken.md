# Boneless Korean fried chicken

## Character and experience

Boneless Korean fried chicken is a format variant made from bite-size boneless chicken pieces; it can be sold plain or with many seasonings.

The base is savory and fried, while the chosen sauce or powder determines sweetness, spice and aroma.

Small pieces provide a high ratio of crisp coating to tender chicken and are easy to eat without bones.

It is served hot and cools faster than large bone-in pieces.

A box is a filling main or shareable snack.

## Context and comparisons

Boneless chicken is a widespread Korean delivery format valued for convenience, but it is not one fixed flavor.

It is Korean fried chicken cut into easy-to-share bites rather than a separate sauce category.

## Ingredients and variations

Boneless chicken and frying oil define the format. Coating and seasoning vary, so the menu's flavor concept must supply additional facts.

Wheat, egg, soy and milk are possible in coating or seasoning. Boneless status does not reduce allergen or shared-fryer uncertainty.
