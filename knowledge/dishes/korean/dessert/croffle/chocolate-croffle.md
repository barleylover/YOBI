# Chocolate croffle

## Overview
Chocolate croffle is a croffle variant defined by chocolate sauce, spread, chips or cocoa topping.

## Taste
It is buttery, toasted, sweet and cocoa-rich.

## Texture
Crisp ridges and flaky-chewy dough contrast with smooth or firm chocolate toppings.

## Temperature
It is served warm or at room temperature, sometimes with cold ice cream.

## Satiety
Chocolate and rich pastry make it a substantial dessert.

## Culture
It is a common flavored croffle category across Korean cafes.

## Analogy
It is a waffle-pressed croissant finished as a chocolate dessert.

## Ingredients
Wheat dough and chocolate define the variant. Butter, cream, ice cream, nuts and syrup may appear.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Milk, soy, egg, tree nuts and peanuts are possible through dough, chocolate and toppings.
