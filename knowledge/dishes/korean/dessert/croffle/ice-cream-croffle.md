# Ice cream croffle

## Character and experience

Ice cream croffle is a croffle variant defined by one or more scoops of ice cream served on the warm pressed pastry.

It is buttery, toasted, creamy and sweet, with flavor determined by the ice cream and sauce.

Warm crisp-chewy pastry contrasts with cold smooth ice cream.

It intentionally combines a warm croffle with frozen ice cream.

Pastry and ice cream make it a rich dessert suitable for sharing.

## Context and comparisons

It is a stable Korean cafe presentation of croffle rather than a merchant-specific creation.

It is a waffle-pressed croissant served à la mode.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat croissant dough and dairy ice cream define the variant. Egg, syrup, chocolate, fruit and nuts may appear.

Wheat and milk are presumed present. Egg, soy, tree nuts and peanuts are possible through dough, ice cream and toppings.
