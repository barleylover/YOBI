# Croffle

## Character and experience

Croffle is a cafe-dessert family made by pressing croissant-style laminated dough in a waffle iron.

It is buttery, toasted and mildly sweet, with toppings determining additional flavor.

The surface is crisp and ridged while the layered interior remains chewy or flaky.

It is served warm or at room temperature, often with a cold topping.

One piece is a rich snack or dessert.

## Context and comparisons

Croffles became a stable Korean cafe menu category rather than a single merchant product.

It is croissant dough cooked in the shape and iron pattern of a waffle.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat croissant dough defines the family. Butter, egg, sugar, ice cream, fruit, nuts and sauces vary.

Wheat is presumed present. Milk, egg, soy and tree nuts are possible through dough and toppings.
