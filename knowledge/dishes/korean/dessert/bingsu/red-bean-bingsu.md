# Red bean bingsu

## Character and experience

Red bean bingsu is a bingsu variant defined by a generous topping of cooked sweetened red beans.

It is sweet and earthy, often enriched by milk and balanced by neutral ice.

Fine ice contrasts with soft whole beans and optional chewy rice cake.

It is served frozen.

Beans, dairy and rice-cake toppings make it more substantial than fruit-only shaved ice.

## Context and comparisons

Patbingsu is the traditional reference variant of Korean bingsu and a familiar summer dessert.

It is shaved ice crowned with sweet red beans as the primary flavor.

## Ingredients and variations

Shaved ice and red bean define the variant. Milk, condensed milk, rice cake, nuts and cereal are possible.

Milk, tree nuts, peanuts and soy are possible through the base and toppings; red bean itself does not establish those allergens.
