# Mango bingsu

## Character and experience

Mango bingsu is a bingsu variant defined by fresh, frozen or pureed mango as the main topping.

It is sweet, fruity and tangy, often with creamy milk notes.

Soft mango pieces and syrup contrast with fine shaved ice.

It is served frozen and softens quickly as fruit warms.

It is a shared dessert; dairy, ice cream or cake toppings increase richness.

## Context and comparisons

Mango bingsu is a widespread modern Korean cafe variation of the shaved-ice tradition.

It is a mango dessert built on snow-like shaved ice.

## Ingredients and variations

Shaved ice and mango define the variant. Milk, condensed milk, ice cream, syrup and nuts may appear.

Milk, tree nuts, peanuts and soy are possible through the base and cafe toppings.
