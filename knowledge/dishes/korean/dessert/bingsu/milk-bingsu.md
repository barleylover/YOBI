# Milk bingsu

## Character and experience

Milk bingsu is a bingsu variant defined by snow-like shaved frozen milk or a clearly dairy-rich base.

It is creamy, sweet and mild, allowing fruit, bean or syrup toppings to stand out.

The base is fine, soft and snow-like rather than coarse water ice.

It is served frozen and melts quickly.

Dairy makes it richer than plain water-ice bingsu, though it remains a shared dessert.

## Context and comparisons

Milk snow bingsu is a stable modern Korean cafe category and a common base for multiple toppings.

It is frozen milk shaved into a soft dessert mound rather than milk poured over ordinary ice.

## Ingredients and variations

Milk-based shaved ice defines the variant. Condensed milk, ice cream, fruit, nuts and cake pieces may appear.

Milk is presumed present. Tree nuts, peanuts, soy and wheat are possible through toppings.
