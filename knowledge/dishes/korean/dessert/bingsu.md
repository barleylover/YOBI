# Bingsu

## Character and experience

Bingsu is a Korean dessert family of finely shaved ice or frozen milk base served with sweet toppings.

It is cold and sweet, with the topping determining fruit, bean, chocolate or nut flavors.

Fine ice ranges from crystalline to snow-like, contrasted with soft, chewy or crisp toppings.

It is served frozen and melts quickly.

A bowl is usually a shared dessert, with dairy and dense toppings increasing richness.

## Context and comparisons

Bingsu is a central Korean cafe dessert, especially popular in warm weather.

It is a shareable mound of flavored shaved ice whose topping defines the subtype.

## Ingredients and variations

Shaved ice defines the family. Milk, condensed milk, fruit, red bean, rice cake, nuts and syrups vary.

Milk, tree nuts, peanuts and soy are possible through the base and toppings. The visible topping does not reveal the full base recipe.
