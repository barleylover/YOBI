# Tuna gimbap

## Overview
Tuna gimbap is a gimbap variant whose named filling includes tuna, usually alongside vegetables and other standard roll fillings.

## Taste
It is savory and mild, with a richer fish filling that may be made creamy with mayonnaise and balanced by pickled vegetables.

## Texture
Flaked tuna adds a soft, dense filling inside the chewy rice, flexible seaweed and crisp or tender vegetables inherited from gimbap.

## Temperature
It is generally served at room temperature and should be kept appropriately chilled or consumed promptly because of the prepared fish filling.

## Satiety
One roll is a light meal or substantial snack, with the tuna filling making it richer than a simple vegetable roll.

## Culture
Tuna gimbap is a common convenience-store, snack-shop and packed-lunch variation of the Korean portable rice roll.

## Analogy
It resembles a tuna-filled rice pinwheel, but its seasoned rice, seaweed and Korean vegetable fillings distinguish it from a tuna sandwich or sushi roll.

## Ingredients
Tuna defines this variant, while rice and seaweed are inherited from gimbap. Mayonnaise, egg, pickled radish and vegetables are common recipe choices rather than guarantees.

## Safety
Fish is presumed present. Egg is possible through mayonnaise or an egg strip, while inherited soy and sesame risks and merchant-specific cross-contact still require confirmation.
