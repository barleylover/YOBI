# Kimchi gimbap

## Character and experience

Kimchi gimbap is a gimbap variant in which seasoned kimchi is a prominent named filling rather than a minor accompaniment.

It is tangy, savory and usually spicy, balanced by mild rice and nutty seaweed.

Soft rice and flexible seaweed surround crisp-tender or softened kimchi and optional fillings.

It is generally served at room temperature.

One roll is a light meal or substantial snack.

## Context and comparisons

Kimchi gimbap is a familiar Korean snack-shop variation that brings a staple fermented side dish into a portable roll.

It is a rice-and-seaweed roll whose central flavor comes from fermented kimchi.

## Ingredients and variations

Rice, seaweed and kimchi define the variant. Egg, fish cake, vegetables, pork or chili sauce may also appear.

Fish or shellfish seasoning may occur in kimchi, while soy, wheat and sesame may enter through sauces and inherited gimbap components.
