# Cheese gimbap

## Overview
Cheese gimbap is a gimbap variant containing cheese as a named filling alongside the rice, seaweed and varied fillings of the parent dish.

## Taste
Cheese adds salty, creamy richness to the mild, savory and lightly nutty profile of gimbap.

## Texture
The cheese may feel soft or slightly firm depending on type and temperature, contrasting with chewy rice and crisp vegetable fillings.

## Temperature
It is generally served at room temperature, though some shops briefly warm the roll and soften the cheese.

## Satiety
One roll is a light meal or substantial snack, and cheese makes it somewhat richer than a basic vegetable roll.

## Culture
Cheese gimbap is a familiar modern snack-shop variation that adds a dairy filling to the portable Korean rice roll.

## Analogy
It is a cheese-filled rice-and-seaweed pinwheel rather than a melted cheese wrap; the exact cheese format varies by shop.

## Ingredients
Cheese defines this variant, while rice and seaweed are inherited from gimbap. Egg, pickled radish, vegetables and processed fillings remain recipe-specific.

## Safety
Milk is presumed present. Egg, soy and sesame can also occur through inherited common fillings and seasoning, so the exact shop recipe still matters.
