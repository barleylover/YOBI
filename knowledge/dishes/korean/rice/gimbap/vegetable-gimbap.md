# Vegetable gimbap

## Overview
Vegetable gimbap is a gimbap variant centered on several vegetable fillings rather than a named meat or seafood filling.

## Taste
It is mild, savory and lightly sweet or tangy from seasoned and pickled vegetables.

## Texture
Soft rice surrounds a mix of crisp, tender and pickled vegetable textures inside flexible seaweed.

## Temperature
It is normally served at room temperature.

## Satiety
One roll is a light meal, with vegetables making it fresh but still substantial through the rice.

## Culture
It is a common snack-shop and packed-lunch choice, but the name does not automatically mean vegan.

## Analogy
It is a vegetable-forward rice roll whose identity comes from the filling mix rather than one protein.

## Ingredients
Rice, seaweed and mixed vegetables define the variant. Pickled radish, spinach, carrot, egg and sesame oil are common recipe choices.

## Safety
Egg, soy and sesame remain possible. A vegetable label does not prove vegan preparation or absence of animal-derived seasoning.
