# Spicy gimbap

## Character and experience

Spicy gimbap is a broad but recognizable gimbap variant in which a chili-seasoned filling or sauce is the defining feature.

It is savory and chili-hot, often balanced by sweet, tangy or nutty notes.

Chewy rice and flexible seaweed surround fillings that may be crisp, tender or saucy.

It is usually served at room temperature despite its hot flavor profile.

One roll is a light meal or substantial snack.

## Context and comparisons

Snack shops use spicy gimbap as a common contrast to mild standard rolls and as a pairing for soup or tteokbokki.

It is a standard gimbap format with heat built into the filling rather than added only as a dipping sauce.

## Ingredients and variations

Rice, seaweed and a chili-based seasoning define the variant. The protein and vegetable fillings differ widely by recipe.

Soy, wheat, egg and sesame are possible through sauces and fillings. Spicy labeling says nothing about allergy safety or a specific protein.
