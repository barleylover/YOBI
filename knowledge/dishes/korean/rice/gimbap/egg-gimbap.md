# Egg gimbap

## Character and experience

Egg gimbap is a gimbap variant in which cooked egg is an abundant or named filling rather than a minor optional strip.

It is mild, savory and slightly rich, often balanced by pickled vegetables and sesame seasoning.

Soft egg and rice contrast with crisp vegetables and the flexible seaweed wrapper.

It is usually served at room temperature after the egg has been cooked and cooled enough to roll.

Egg adds protein and richness, making one roll a satisfying light meal.

## Context and comparisons

It is a familiar mild gimbap variation suited to lunch boxes, snack shops and diners avoiding strong spice.

It is a rice roll built around an omelet-like filling rather than meat or fish.

## Ingredients and variations

Egg, rice and seaweed define the variant. Vegetables, pickled radish and sesame oil are common additions.

Egg is presumed present. Soy and sesame remain possible through seasonings and inherited gimbap components.
