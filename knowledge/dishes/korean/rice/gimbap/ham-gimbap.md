# Ham gimbap

## Character and experience

Ham gimbap is a gimbap variant in which a clearly identifiable strip of processed ham is the named filling.

It is mild, salty and savory, balanced by seasoned rice and pickled vegetables.

Firm-soft ham sits among chewy rice, flexible seaweed and crisp vegetables.

It is usually served at room temperature.

One roll is a light meal or substantial snack.

## Context and comparisons

Ham gimbap is a long-established convenience-store, lunch-box and snack-shop variation.

It is a classic gimbap roll made meat-forward by a simple ham strip.

## Ingredients and variations

Rice, seaweed and ham define the variant. Egg, pickled radish, vegetables and sesame oil are common.

Soy, wheat or milk may occur in processed ham, while egg and sesame are possible through other fillings and seasoning.
