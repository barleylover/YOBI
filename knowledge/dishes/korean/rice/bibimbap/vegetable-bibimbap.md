# Vegetable bibimbap

## Overview
Vegetable bibimbap is a bibimbap variant that emphasizes an assortment of seasoned vegetables over a named meat topping.

## Taste
It balances earthy, fresh, sweet and savory vegetable flavors, often with nutty sesame notes and optional sweet-hot gochujang.

## Texture
Soft rice contrasts with tender, crisp and chewy vegetables, while an optional egg can add a soft or runny element.

## Temperature
It is commonly served warm, although individual vegetable toppings and some regional presentations may be cool.

## Satiety
A full bowl is a complete one-person meal, with egg, tofu or extra rice changing its richness.

## Culture
The dish keeps bibimbap's central practice of arranging varied colors and textures before mixing them thoroughly at the table.

## Analogy
Think of a Korean mixed grain bowl centered on several individually seasoned vegetables rather than one featured meat.

## Ingredients
Rice is inherited from bibimbap and assorted seasoned vegetables define this variant. Gochujang, sesame oil and egg remain common rather than guaranteed.

## Safety
The word vegetable does not prove vegan status: egg, fish-based seasoning or animal-derived broth may still occur. Soy and sesame are possible, and merchant evidence must determine the actual recipe.
