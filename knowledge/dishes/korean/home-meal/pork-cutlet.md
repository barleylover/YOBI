# Korean pork cutlet

## Overview
Korean pork cutlet is a family of breaded, fried pork cutlets commonly served sliced with rice, cabbage and a brown sauce.

## Taste
It is savory and fried, with sweet-tangy sauce and fresh cabbage providing contrast.

## Texture
The defining texture is crisp breading around tender pork, usually paired with soft rice.

## Temperature
The cutlet is served hot; salad and some sides are cool.

## Satiety
A cutlet with rice and sides is a filling main meal.

## Culture
Korean donkkaseu adapts Japanese-style breaded cutlet into snack-shop, family-restaurant and home-meal formats.

## Analogy
It resembles a crisp breaded pork steak served as a composed rice plate.

## Ingredients
Pork and wheat breading define the family. Egg wash, milk, sauce, rice and cabbage are common.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Egg, milk and soy are possible in breading or sauce, and shared fryer risk must be resolved by merchant data.
