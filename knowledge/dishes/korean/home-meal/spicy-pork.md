# Spicy stir-fried pork

## Character and experience

Spicy stir-fried pork is a Korean dish family of sliced pork cooked quickly with a red chili-based seasoning and vegetables.

It is savory, sweet and chili-hot, with garlic and fermented seasoning adding depth.

Tender or lightly charred pork combines with softened onion and a clingy sauce.

It is served hot from the pan.

With rice it forms a hearty full meal; alone it is a substantial shared main.

## Context and comparisons

Jeyuk-bokkeum is a familiar home-style, lunch-set and workplace-cafeteria dish.

It is a saucy Korean pork stir-fry designed to be eaten with rice.

## Ingredients and variations

Pork and chili seasoning define the family. Onion, cabbage, garlic, soy sauce and sesame are common.

Soy, wheat and sesame are possible in gochujang, soy sauce and finishing oil. Pork is presumed present.
