# Kimchi-jjigae baekban

## Character and experience

Kimchi-jjigae baekban is a set meal in which kimchi stew is the named main, accompanied by rice and several side dishes.

It is tangy, savory and often spicy, with side dishes balancing the strong fermented stew.

Soft kimchi, tofu and optional pork sit in broth beside rice and assorted banchan textures.

The stew and rice are served hot; most side dishes are cool or room temperature.

Rice, stew and side dishes form a complete meal.

## Context and comparisons

It is one of the most familiar Korean home-style lunch-set formats.

It is kimchi stew presented as the center of a full rice set rather than as a standalone pot.

## Ingredients and variations

Kimchi, rice and side dishes define the set. Pork, tofu, seafood seasoning and changing banchan are possible.

Soy, fish, shellfish, wheat and sesame are possible across stew and sides. Kimchi does not by itself reveal whether seafood seasoning was used.
