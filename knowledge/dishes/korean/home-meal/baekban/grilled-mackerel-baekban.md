# Grilled mackerel baekban

## Overview
Grilled mackerel baekban is a baekban variant centered on grilled mackerel with rice, soup and assorted side dishes.

## Taste
It is savory, briny and rich, balanced by plain rice and fresh or fermented banchan.

## Texture
Flaky fish and crisp skin contrast with soft rice and varied side dishes.

## Temperature
The fish, rice and soup are served hot or warm; banchan may be cool.

## Satiety
It is a complete, moderately rich set meal.

## Culture
Grilled-fish baekban is a familiar Korean home-meal and specialist restaurant format.

## Analogy
It is grilled mackerel presented as the protein center of a full Korean rice set.

## Ingredients
Mackerel, rice and assorted side dishes define the set. Soup, soy seasoning and sesame vary.

## Safety
Fish is presumed present. Soy, wheat, sesame and shellfish risk are possible across seasoning and rotating sides.
