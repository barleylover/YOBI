# Grilled pork baekban

## Character and experience

Grilled pork baekban is a baekban variant centered on sliced grilled or griddled pork, often called dwaeji bulbaek, with rice and side dishes.

It is savory and smoky, with either soy-sweet or red chili seasoning depending on the preparation.

Tender or lightly charred pork contrasts with soft rice and varied banchan.

The pork and rice are served hot; side dishes span cool to warm.

It is a hearty complete meal with a protein-heavy main.

## Context and comparisons

Dwaeji bulbaek is a familiar quick Korean set meal found in home-style restaurants and driver-oriented eateries.

It is a compact Korean barbecue-style pork meal served as a ready rice set.

## Ingredients and variations

Pork, rice and assorted side dishes define the variant. Soy sauce, chili paste, garlic and sesame are possible seasonings.

Soy, wheat, sesame and shellfish risk are possible across marinades and banchan. Pork is presumed present.
