# Bulgogi baekban

## Character and experience

Bulgogi baekban is a baekban variant whose named main is Korean marinated beef, served with rice and a changing set of side dishes.

The beef is typically savory and gently sweet, while side dishes add fermented, fresh and spicy contrasts.

Tender sliced beef accompanies soft rice and a range of side-dish textures.

The beef, rice and soup are served hot or warm; side dishes may be cool.

It is a complete and filling set meal.

## Context and comparisons

The format presents familiar bulgogi as the centerpiece of an everyday Korean rice set.

It is bulgogi with a full rice-and-banchan meal around it rather than bulgogi alone.

## Ingredients and variations

Beef, rice and assorted side dishes define the set. Soy marinade, sesame, vegetables and soup commonly accompany it.

Soy, wheat, sesame and egg are possible across marinade and side dishes. The rotating set must be checked at menu level.
