# Spicy pork baekban

## Overview
Spicy pork baekban is a baekban variant whose named main is jeyuk-style chili-seasoned pork, served with rice and side dishes.

## Taste
It is savory, sweet and spicy, balanced by plain rice and varied banchan.

## Texture
Tender or lightly charred pork contrasts with soft rice and multiple side-dish textures.

## Temperature
The pork, rice and soup are hot or warm; side dishes may be cool.

## Satiety
It is a hearty, complete set meal.

## Culture
Jeyuk baekban is a common Korean home-style restaurant and workplace-lunch format.

## Analogy
It is spicy pork stir-fry expanded into a full rice-and-banchan set.

## Ingredients
Pork, chili seasoning, rice and assorted sides define the variant. Soy sauce, sesame and rotating soup ingredients vary.

## Safety
Soy, wheat, sesame, fish and shellfish are possible across marinade, soup and side dishes.
