# Korean baekban

## Overview
Korean baekban is a set-meal family consisting of rice with several small side dishes and usually a soup, stew or named main.

## Taste
The meal balances salty, savory, fermented, fresh and sometimes spicy flavors across separate dishes.

## Texture
Because it is a set, textures range from soft rice and soup to crisp vegetables, chewy proteins and pickles.

## Temperature
Rice and soup are served warm or hot, while many side dishes are cool or room temperature.

## Satiety
A full set is designed as a complete, filling meal.

## Culture
Baekban represents everyday Korean home-style dining in which rice anchors multiple shared or individual side dishes.

## Analogy
It is a composed rice set rather than one mixed bowl, with variety coming from several small dishes.

## Ingredients
Rice and assorted side dishes define the family. The soup, main protein and banchan rotate by set and merchant.

## Safety
Soy, wheat, egg, fish, shellfish and sesame can appear across different side dishes. A family-level Wiki cannot establish safety for every component in a set.
