# Grilled mackerel

## Overview
Grilled mackerel is a Korean fish dish centered on a split or portioned mackerel cooked until browned, often served with rice and side dishes.

## Taste
It is savory, briny and rich, with charred skin and optional salt or soy seasoning.

## Texture
The skin can become crisp while the oily flesh remains flaky and moist.

## Temperature
It is served hot or warm.

## Satiety
With rice and sides it is a complete meal; the fish alone is a moderate main.

## Culture
Grilled mackerel is a common home-meal and baekban main valued for straightforward preparation and strong flavor.

## Analogy
It is a simple grilled fish main whose identity comes from mackerel rather than a complex sauce.

## Ingredients
Mackerel defines the dish. Salt, oil, lemon, soy sauce or sesame seasoning are optional.

## Safety
Fish is presumed present. Soy, wheat and sesame are possible only through optional seasoning or shared preparation.
