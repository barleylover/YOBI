# Gireum tteokbokki

## Character and experience

Gireum tteokbokki is a drier tteokbokki variant in which rice cakes are oil-coated and pan-fried with seasoning.

It is savory, toasted and often chili-spicy, with less sweet liquid sauce than standard tteokbokki.

The rice cakes remain chewy while direct pan contact creates lightly crisp or browned surfaces.

It is served hot or warm from the pan.

A portion is a substantial snack and feels less stew-like than regular tteokbokki.

## Context and comparisons

It is associated with traditional market-style rice-cake snacks and is a recognized subtype rather than a merchant invention.

It is a seasoned rice-cake stir-fry instead of rice cakes simmered in red sauce.

## Ingredients and variations

Rice cakes and cooking oil define the variant. Chili powder, soy sauce, sugar and sesame may be used.

Soy, wheat and sesame are possible through seasoning. The dry appearance does not establish allergen absence.
