# Rabokki

## Overview
Rabokki is a tteokbokki variant that combines rice cakes with instant ramyeon-style noodles in the same seasoned pan.

## Taste
It is usually sweet, savory and spicy, with noodle seasoning adding a deeper salty note.

## Texture
Chewy rice cakes and springy noodles provide two distinct starch textures in a thick sauce.

## Temperature
It is served piping hot and is best eaten before the noodles soften too far.

## Satiety
The combined rice cakes and noodles make it a filling snack or casual one-dish meal.

## Culture
Rabokki is a classic Korean bunsik combination that joins two inexpensive comfort foods in one serving.

## Analogy
It is tteokbokki with noodles integrated into the dish, not merely served alongside it.

## Ingredients
Rice cakes and wheat noodles define rabokki. Red seasoning, fish cake, egg and vegetables are frequent additions rather than universal facts.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present through the noodles. Soy and egg are possible through seasoning, fish cake or toppings; exact menu facts take priority.
