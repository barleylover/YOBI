# Cheese tteokbokki

## Overview
Cheese tteokbokki is a common tteokbokki variant finished with melted cheese over rice cakes and sauce. The cheese is part of the food type, while the sauce style can vary.

## Taste
It combines the sweet, savory and spicy profile of tteokbokki with a creamy, salty dairy note that softens perceived heat.

## Texture
Chewy rice cakes contrast with stretchy or molten cheese and a thick coating sauce.

## Temperature
It is served hot so the cheese remains melted and the rice cakes stay tender.

## Satiety
Cheese adds richness, making one portion more filling than plain tteokbokki.

## Culture
It is a familiar Korean snack-shop and delivery variation designed for diners who want a milder, richer tteokbokki experience.

## Analogy
It is like spicy-sweet rice cakes under a melted cheese topping rather than a separate cheese side dish.

## Ingredients
Rice cakes and cheese define the variant. Gochujang sauce, sugar, fish cake or vegetables are common but depend on the recipe.

## Safety
Milk is presumed present because cheese defines the dish. Soy or wheat may occur in sauce and fish-cake components; merchant data must confirm a specific menu.
