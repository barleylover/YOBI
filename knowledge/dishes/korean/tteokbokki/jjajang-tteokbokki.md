# Jjajang tteokbokki

## Overview
Jjajang tteokbokki coats Korean rice cakes in a dark roasted-bean sauce inspired by jjajangmyeon instead of relying only on red chili sauce.

## Taste
The sauce is savory, gently sweet and roasted, usually less chili-forward than classic red tteokbokki.

## Texture
Chewy rice cakes carry a smooth, thick sauce, with onion or cabbage sometimes adding softness or crunch.

## Temperature
It is served hot and thickens as it cools.

## Satiety
A bowl is a substantial snack and becomes a meal when noodles, egg or fried sides are added.

## Culture
This snack-shop variant adapts Korean-Chinese jjajang flavor to a familiar tteokbokki format.

## Analogy
It resembles jjajangmyeon sauce paired with chewy rice cakes rather than noodles.

## Ingredients
Rice cakes and black bean paste define the variant. Onion, cabbage, sugar and oil are common supporting ingredients.

## Safety
Soy is presumed present through black bean paste. Wheat is possible in the paste, sauce or added noodles, so a specific menu still needs confirmation.
