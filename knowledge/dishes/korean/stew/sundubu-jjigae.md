# Sundubu jjigae

## Character and experience

Sundubu jjigae is a bubbling Korean stew built around very soft unpressed tofu. Broth and protein choices vary by named version.

It is savory and often chilli-hot, with deeper seafood or meat notes depending on the broth.

Silky tofu breaks into soft curds in the spoon, contrasted with vegetables, meat or shellfish.

It arrives extremely hot and continues cooking briefly in a stone or metal pot.

With rice, one pot is a complete and warming individual meal.

## Context and comparisons

The dish is valued for dramatic heat and soft texture; restaurants often let diners choose seafood, meat or mixed versions.

It is a spicy, brothier tofu stew whose curds are softer than standard cubed tofu.

## Ingredients and variations

Soft tofu and broth define the dish. Chilli seasoning is common; shellfish, pork, beef and egg depend on the version.

Soy is presumed present. Shellfish, egg and meat are possible, and broth plus shared-pot cross-contact need menu-specific confirmation.
