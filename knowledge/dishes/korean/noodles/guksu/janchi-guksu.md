# Janchi guksu

## Character and experience

Janchi guksu is a warm Korean noodle-soup variant made with thin wheat noodles in a clear, light broth and simple garnishes.

It is mild, clean and savory, with soy seasoning or kimchi sometimes adding depth.

Soft-springy noodles sit in a light broth with tender vegetable and egg garnishes.

It is served hot or warm.

It is a light-to-moderate meal and can accompany other dishes.

## Context and comparisons

Its name refers to festive banquet noodles, though it is now common everyday comfort food.

It is a simple warm noodle soup whose appeal comes from clear broth and delicate toppings.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat noodles and broth define the variant. Anchovy stock, vegetables, egg and soy seasoning are common.

Wheat is presumed present. Fish, egg, soy and sesame are possible through broth and garnish.
