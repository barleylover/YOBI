# Chicken kalguksu

## Character and experience

Chicken kalguksu is a hot Korean noodle soup centered on broad knife-cut wheat noodles and chicken broth. Garnishes and broth seasoning vary by kitchen.

The usual profile is savory, gentle and broth-forward, with chicken depth rather than strong chilli heat.

The broad noodles are soft-chewy and slightly rustic; shredded chicken adds tender fibres.

It is normally delivered steaming hot and can continue softening in the broth during transit.

Noodles, broth and chicken make it a substantial one-person meal.

## Context and comparisons

Kalguksu is familiar Korean comfort food, especially welcome in cool or rainy weather; chicken is one of several broth styles.

It is closest to chicken noodle soup, but with thicker hand-cut-style noodles and a less herb-heavy broth.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat noodles define the dish, while chicken and chicken broth form the core. Garlic, scallion, zucchini or potato may appear but are not universal.

Wheat is presumed present from the defining noodles. Broth seasoning and kitchen cross-contact still require menu-specific evidence.
