# Bibim naengmyeon

## Character and experience

Bibim naengmyeon is a brothless naengmyeon variant in which cold noodles are mixed with a concentrated red chili sauce.

It is sweet, tangy, savory and distinctly spicy.

Firm, elastic cold noodles are coated in thick sauce with crisp vegetables.

It is served cold, usually without the large volume of icy broth found in mul naengmyeon.

One bowl is a moderate but flavor-dense meal.

## Context and comparisons

It is a standard counterpart to mul naengmyeon in Korean noodle restaurants and barbecue meals.

It is a cold spicy noodle salad rather than a cold noodle soup.

## Ingredients and variations

Cold noodles and chili sauce define the variant. Radish, cucumber, egg and meat slices are possible.

Wheat, soy, sesame and egg are possible through noodles, sauce and toppings.
