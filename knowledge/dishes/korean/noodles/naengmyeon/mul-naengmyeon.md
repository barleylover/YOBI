# Mul naengmyeon

## Overview
Mul naengmyeon is the broth-based naengmyeon variant, serving cold noodles in a chilled savory-tangy broth.

## Taste
It is refreshing, mildly savory, tangy and sometimes gently sweet or mustard-sharp.

## Texture
Firm, slippery noodles sit in thin cold broth with crisp radish or cucumber.

## Temperature
It is served very cold, often with ice in the broth.

## Satiety
One bowl is a moderate meal that feels light because of its cold broth.

## Culture
It is a prominent Korean summer dish and also a classic follow-up to grilled meat.

## Analogy
It is a cold noodle soup rather than a dressed noodle salad.

## Ingredients
Cold noodles and chilled broth define the variant. Radish, cucumber, egg, beef and mustard are common additions.

## Safety
Wheat, egg, soy and sesame are possible. Broth may contain animal products even when toppings appear simple.
