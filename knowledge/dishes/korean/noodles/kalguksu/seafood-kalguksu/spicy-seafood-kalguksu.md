# Spicy seafood kalguksu

## Overview
Spicy seafood kalguksu is a seafood kalguksu subtype defined by both mixed seafood and a distinctly chili-hot broth.

## Taste
It is briny, savory, garlicky and spicy.

## Texture
Chewy broad noodles combine with tender or springy seafood in broth.

## Temperature
It is served piping hot.

## Satiety
Noodles and seafood make it a substantial full meal.

## Culture
It is a broadly recognized Korean noodle-shop variation for diners seeking the seafood bowl with stronger heat.

## Analogy
It is seafood kalguksu pushed toward the flavor intensity of a spicy seafood stew.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles, seafood, broth and chili seasoning define the variant. Shellfish types, squid and vegetables vary.

## Safety
Wheat and shellfish risk are presumed present. Fish and soy are possible through seafood mix and broth.
