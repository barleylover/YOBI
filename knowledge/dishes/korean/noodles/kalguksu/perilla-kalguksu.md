# Perilla kalguksu

## Character and experience

Perilla kalguksu is a kalguksu variant defined by ground perilla seed that thickens and flavors the broth.

It is nutty, earthy, savory and usually mild rather than chili-hot.

Chewy knife-cut noodles sit in a creamy, slightly grainy broth.

It is served steaming hot.

The dense seed-enriched broth makes it a filling noodle meal.

## Context and comparisons

Deulkkae kalguksu is a stable Korean noodle-shop and home-style category valued for mellow richness.

It is knife-cut noodle soup made creamy through ground seeds rather than dairy cream.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat noodles, broth and perilla seed powder define the variant. Mushrooms, potato and vegetables are common.

Wheat is presumed present. Soy, sesame and tree nuts are possible through broth, garnishes or shared seed handling; perilla itself is distinct from sesame.
