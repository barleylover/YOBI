# Seafood kalguksu

## Character and experience

Seafood kalguksu is a kalguksu variant whose broth and topping prominently include shellfish or mixed seafood.

It is briny, savory and clean, often with garlic and mild pepper or chili.

Soft-chewy knife-cut noodles sit with tender or springy seafood in broth.

It is served steaming hot.

Noodles and seafood make it a complete, filling bowl.

## Context and comparisons

It is a common Korean coastal and casual noodle-shop variation of kalguksu.

It is knife-cut noodle soup enriched by a mixed seafood pot.

## Ingredients and variations

Wheat noodles, broth and seafood define the variant. Clams, mussels, shrimp, squid, garlic and vegetables vary.

Wheat and shellfish risk are presumed present. Fish and soy are possible through broth or seasoning; the exact seafood mix remains menu-specific.
