# Bibim kalguksu

## Overview
Bibim kalguksu is a brothless or lightly sauced kalguksu variant in which boiled knife-cut noodles are mixed with a red seasoned sauce.

## Taste
It is tangy, sweet, savory and usually chili-hot.

## Texture
Broad chewy noodles are coated in sauce and contrasted with crisp vegetables.

## Temperature
It is commonly served cool or at room temperature after draining.

## Satiety
The dense noodles make it a filling one-bowl meal.

## Culture
It applies the Korean bibim noodle style to the broader, handmade-looking kalguksu noodle.

## Analogy
It is knife-cut noodles dressed like bibim-guksu rather than served in soup.

## Ingredients
Wheat noodles and mixed chili sauce define the variant. Cucumber, cabbage, egg and sesame are common.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Soy, sesame and egg are possible through sauce and toppings.
