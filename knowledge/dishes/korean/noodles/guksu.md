# Korean wheat noodles

## Overview
Korean wheat noodles is a broad family of thin wheat-noodle dishes served either in broth or mixed with a seasoned sauce.

## Taste
The base noodle is mild, while broth or sauce determines whether the dish is delicate, savory, tangy or spicy.

## Texture
The noodles are slender, soft-springy and easy to slurp.

## Temperature
Variants range from hot broth bowls to cool mixed noodles.

## Satiety
A bowl is a light-to-moderate meal, with toppings affecting richness.

## Culture
Guksu is everyday Korean noodle food associated with simple meals, celebrations and seasonal dishes.

## Analogy
It is a shared noodle foundation whose serving liquid or sauce creates the named variant.

## Ingredients
Thin wheat noodles define the family. Broth, sauce, vegetables, egg, anchovy stock and sesame vary by variant.

## Safety
Wheat is presumed present. Soy, egg, fish and sesame are possible and must be resolved by the named variant.
